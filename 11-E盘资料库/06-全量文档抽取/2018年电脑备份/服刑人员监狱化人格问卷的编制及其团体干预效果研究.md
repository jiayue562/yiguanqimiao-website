Abstract
Jail-rization personality was an important issue. As a special environment, prison played an indelible negative role in the developing of personality of the prisoners, and even bears the brand of “prison”, thus formed jail-rization personality. Jail-rization personality meaned that under the conditions of imprisonment in a long career, it was the results of multiple factors combined. The prisoners adapted to the imprisonment life and it was a personality variation in compliance with distinctive behavior pattern, specific mode of thinking , distinctive emotional reactions characterists. performance Once the Prisoners’ jail-rization personality was formed, it would bring a range of hazards, would increase the difficulty for reform of criminals,and also increase the difficulty for prisoners to adapt to society , and thereby increase the social factors of disharmony.
In order to develope a prisoners’ prisonized personality questionnaire , analyze the male prisoners’ prisonized personality characteristics and to explore the the grouping interventon tactics to cure the prisonized personality , we designed three studies:study one based on summarizing the documents investigating of opened-ended questionnaire visiting the male prisoners,officers and discussing with psychology experts the theoretics hypothesis was constructed.According to it the study designed a questiounaire of prisoners’ prisonized personality;study two in order to investigate prisoners to find some characters of them, 1765 male prisoners was tested by questiounaire of prisoners’ prisonized personality;in study three we have chosen 10 male prisoners who with high scores of prisonized personality to carry out group training,and have joined a check out interfering with effect.The main results of three study were as follows:
(1)The self-made questionnaire of this study was entirely accord with the request of psychometrics.This questionaire had good reliability and validity. The α of total jail-rization personality questionnaire was 0.930 The contents of the questionnaire corresponded to the reality of the prisoners’ prisonized personality,which could be used as a specialized
III

personality measure tool in measuring the prisonized personality of prisoners . (2) Prisoners’ prisonized personality was constituted by the eight dimensions as
following: pessimistic thought, lowly-trust, antagonism, indifferent, passive, lowly-self-esteem, lying-nature , dual nature. Demonstration model and theoretic model were entirely fitting.
(3)The characteristics of the prisonized personality for the male prisoners was distinct.As a whole lowly-self-esteem was the highest antagonism was the second,which was followed by pessimistic thought indifferent, lying-nature , lowly-trust , passive;dual nature is the lowest one.
(4) Differences of dimensionality in corporate were as following: The dual nature score of the prisoners who were in the prison for between 5 years and 10 years were significantly higher than that of prisoners who were in the prison for under 5years and more than 10years in prison, but there was no significant difference between prisoners who were in the prison for under 5years and more than 10years. The antagonism score and indifferent score of prisoners who served a force personnel sentencewas higher than that of prisoners who served a property personnel sentence. Other dimension had no significant difference.
(5) The results of the intervention were as following : On the condition of that the pre-test result of the intervenient group and the controlled group had no significant difference.After intervention ,compared with the pre-test result the sore of the post-test result of the intervenient group had dropped significantly, At the same time,the post-test and the pre-test of of the control group had no significant diffence.
