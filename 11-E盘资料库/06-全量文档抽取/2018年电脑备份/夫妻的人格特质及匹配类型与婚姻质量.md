Ｒelationship between personality match types and marriage quality in married couples

WANG Zhong-Jie，WANG Yu-Zhong，ZHAO Jiang-Tao，JIA Li-Zhai，GUO Na，ZHANG Hai-Tao
Department of Education Zhengzhou University，Zhengzhou 450001 ，China Corresponding author: WANG Yu-Zhong，zhongjie1597@ zzu. edu. cn

【Abstract】Objective: To explore the relationship between personality match type and marriage quality in married couples，and to provide experimental supports for the improvement of marriage quality，guide and consultation for future marriage. Methods: The Chinese Seven Personality Index ( CSPI) and Marriage Perception Scale ( MPS) were used in a survey of 926 married couples from 14 provinces in China. The couples＇ age ranged from 19 to 70，and their marriage ages were between 1 and 45 years. By means of subgroup regression，the moderating effect of personality match types was analyzed. Ｒesults: The CSPI emotionality factor scores were negatively correlated with the MPS total scores ( r = － 0. 31 － － 0. 41，P ＜ 0. 01) ，while the CSPI factor scores of creativity，persistence，virtue，amenity，husband＇ s extroversion and wife＇ s tradition were positively correlated with the MPS total scores ( r = 0. 18 － 0. 40，P ＜ 0. 01) . Difference of people proportion in high-score group and low -score group of MPS was significant on all the nine match types of couple CSPI ( P ＜ 0. 05) . Couples＇ personality match type was a moderator variable between the relationship of personality and marriage quality ( B = － 2. 50 － 2. 24) . Conclusion: It suggests that couples personalities may be moderately correlated with their marriage quality. Couples＇ personality match types may have moderating effect on the relationship between couple personality traits and marriage quality.
【Key words】 married couples; personality; match types; marriage quality; moderator variables; cross-sectional studies
( Chin Ment Health J，2014，28( 3) : 221 － 226. )
