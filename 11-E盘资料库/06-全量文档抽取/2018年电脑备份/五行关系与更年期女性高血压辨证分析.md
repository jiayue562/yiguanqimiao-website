Five element theory with differentiation of symptoms and signs analysis of female climacteric hypertension

AN Le-jun, QU Man Gu Li, LIN Xue
( Department of Hypertension, Traditional Chinese Medical hospital Afﬁliated to Xinjiang Medical University, Urumqi 830000, China )

Abstract: The Female climacteric hypertension is hypertension in female specific periods. This article analyze its
pathogenesis from unique physiological and pathological process. There are ﬁve aspects of the pathogenesis of Female climacteric hypertension: illness of mother viscera affecting the child one, child-organ disease involving its mother-organ, excessive wood over-restricting earth, disharmony between heart and kidney and hyperactivity of wood counter-restricting metal. By describing mutual generation and restriction of ﬁve element theory to elaborate legislation must proceed from the overall concept in the treatment of female climacteric hypertension. And establish the concept which is “Is the kidney, not limited to the kidney ". It can avoid the imbalance between Yin and Yang and mediate on the balance of yin and Yang, so as to achieve therapeutic effect through harmonizing the liver, kidney, lung, heart and spleen, and paying attention to upper, lower, ascending, descending, and the differentiation of syndromes relationship between Yin, Yang, deﬁciency, excess, in order to prevent the disorder of zang-fu viscera function.
