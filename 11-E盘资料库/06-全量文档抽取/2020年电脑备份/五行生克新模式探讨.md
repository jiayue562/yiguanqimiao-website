Study on new model of ﬁve elements’ generation and restriction
JIN Jiu-cheng1, ZHENG Tao2, HUANG Jian-ping3, GAO Zhi-yuan4, JIN Hao5, SUN Wen-dong1, ZHANG Cheng-bin1, LI Ren6
( 1Institute of Physics and Materials Science, Hunan University, Changsha 410082, China; 2Macao Chinese Medicine Research Institute, Macao 999078, China; 3Xiangya Medical College of Central South University, Changsha 410078, China; 4Henan
Province Xixia First High School, Xixia 474500, China; 5Kimberly-Clark Corporation, TX 75038, USA; 6University of Montreal, Montreal H3H2N7, Canada)

Abstract: For the ﬁrst time, this paper discovered the limitations of the ﬁve elements’ normal generation and normal
restriction model. It doesn’t specify the relationships between ﬁve elements and Yin-Yang, and the essence and operations of ﬁve elements. It also cannot explain the ten pairs of generation interactions among the normal ﬁve zang-organ functions, the generation and restriction interaction phenomenon between two normal generation zang-organs under pathological conditions, and the restriction interaction between two normal restriction zang-organs under pathological conditions.Based on the seven-yao Yin-Yang and heaven and human combining-into-one, a new model of ﬁve element generation and restriction was established. There exists a generation and restriction interaction to each other between any of the two elements. When the generation interaction between the vicinal elements aligns with the direction of the periodic cancellation and increase of Yin-Yang, it is called as normal generation. When the restriction interaction between the separated elements is consistent with the direction of the periodic cancellation and increase of Yin-Yang, it is called as normal restriction. There are 10 pairs of normal generations and 10 pairs of normal restrictions among ﬁve elements.
Key words: Five elements’ normal generation and normal restriction model; Seven-yao Yin-Yang; Yin-Yang with ﬁve
elements; New model of Five elements (viscera) generation and restriction

Abstract: The authors screened the entire texts of the Inner Canon of Huangdi and found out 41 terms concerning the
parts and their positions in the eye area. Of those 41 terms, 14 difﬁcult ones were particularly investigated and the reasons for naming those terms and the meanings of the terms were carefully analyzed. Among the synonyms of each part of the eye area, one term was set up as the standard term. The 41 terms were also classiﬁed into a hierarchy. The authors systematically investigated the people’s knowledge of the parts and their positions of the eye area in the age of the Inner Canon of Huangdi at the ﬁrst time, and laid a theoretical foundation for the further study of the eye theory of the Chinese medicine.
Key words: Inner Canon of Huangdi; Eye area; Morphology; Terminology
