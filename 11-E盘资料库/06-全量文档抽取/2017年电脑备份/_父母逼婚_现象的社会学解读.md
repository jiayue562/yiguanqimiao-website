The Sociological Interpretation of “Forced Marriage of Parents” Yang Jiajia
(School of History and Sociology, Anhui Normal University) Abstract: The "forced marriage" advertisement has been boycotted by publicly, and caused the widespread attention on "parents forced marriage" phenomenon and discussion. "Forced marriage of parents" is a new phenomenon in recent years, and has a profound historical and cultural reasons and realistic social background. From the perspective of sociology to interpretation and analysis of this phenomenon, the purpose is to have a more clear cognition and deep connotation. “Forced marriage of parents” phenomenon is not a social problem, but an intergenerational conflict in marriage with social development to a certain stage and a "Chinese -style marriage anxiety". Key words: Forced Marriages; Marriage Anxiety; Concept of Marriage; Social Problem
